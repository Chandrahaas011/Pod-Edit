# Image Generation Prompts for PodEdit

Status: supplemental research-design material, reviewed 2026-08-11. These
prompts are optional and do not describe the active PodEdit 1.4 implementation.
The canonical measured paper figure is `paper/adaptive_timeline.png`.

The following prompts are designed to generate plain, straightforward, and strictly academic visuals for the PodEdit project. They contain zero creative styling and focus purely on basic, functional diagrams.

---

### 1. The Project Flowchart
**Goal:** To generate a basic academic flowchart mapping the project's data flow.

> **Prompt:** A highly basic flowchart with a plain white background. The chart uses simple, flat rectangular boxes with thin black borders connected by straight downward arrows. The boxes contain basic black text reading: "Raw Video", "Audio Analysis", "Active Speaker Switching", and "Export XML Timeline". Strictly 2D, purely minimal, absolutely no colors, no shading, and no 3D effects. Boring academic textbook style.

---

### 2. The Working Mechanism (Camera Switching Logic)
**Goal:** To show how the software identifies the active speaker plain and simply.

> **Prompt:** A plain 2D schematic diagram on a solid white background. It shows two basic, flat silhouettes of people sitting at a table with microphones. A simple black rectangular outline highlights only one of the silhouettes, with plain text above it reading "Active Speaker Selected". No creative flair, flat vector style, strictly black outlines on white paper, no background details.

---

### 3. Audio Diarization Output Graph
**Goal:** To represent the mathematical timeline of who is speaking.

> **Prompt:** A plain mathematical step-graph with an X-axis representing Time on a pure white background. The graph consists of a single thick black line that steps between two distinct horizontal levels. The top level is labeled "Speaker A" and the bottom level is labeled "Speaker B". Absolutely plain data visualization, standard boring academic chart format, no colors, no gridlines, no creative lighting.

---

### 4. Audio-Video Synchronization Diagram
**Goal:** To show the concept of aligning disparate tracks.

> **Prompt:** A very plain 2D diagram on a flat white background. It features two long flat rectangles stacked horizontally. The top rectangle is labeled "Video Source" and the bottom rectangle is labeled "Audio Source". Extremely thin, straight dashed vertical lines connect the two rectangles perfectly together to indicate synchronization. Stark minimalism, black and grey only, strictly academic blueprint style, no decorative elements.

---

### 5. The Final Output (Timeline Structure)
**Goal:** To show the final staggering of video cuts.

> **Prompt:** A completely flat, basic wireframe diagram of a video editing timeline taking up the center of a white background. The diagram consists only of alternating light grey rectangular blocks placed perfectly next to each other across two rows labeled "Camera 1 Track" and "Camera 2 Track". Below them is one long continuous dark grey block labeled "Master Audio". Plain black text, no software UI gradients, no shadows, extremely boring textbook diagram.
